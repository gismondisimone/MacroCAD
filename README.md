## What is This?
It's a **custom macropad** built with the **Seed Studio XIAO RP2040**, **QMK firmware**. It's design by a CAD user for CAD users. The design is as cool as frigile.

### Features:
- **3x4 Matrix** - Just numbers and a dot key and enter key.
- **OLED Display** - It's a cool add-on though I will update its functions in the future.
- **QMK Firmware** - Open-source, beacuse we like freedom.

---

## How It Works:
This macropad runs **QMK**, which means you get:
- **Custom Keymaps** - Map macros, media keys, or even full-blown scripts.
- **Dynamic Layers** - More functions per button? Yes, please.
- **Display Support** - A cat tha reminds you to work on your project? An integration with Hackatime that swows up your hours spent on desining

**Step 4** - Put two M4x25 screws in the holes
